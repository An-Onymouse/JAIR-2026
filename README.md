# Assessing and Explaining the Persuadability of Large Language Models as Legal Decision Tools

This repository stores all code, data, prompt templates, prompts and statistics relating to the [Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research](https://jair.org) (JAIR) submission named "Assessing and Explaining the Persuadability of Large Language Models as Legal Decision Tools".

If this paper is accepted, this repository will remain available, albeit moved to a non-anonymous repository linked to one of the authors.

## Contents

- [Primary Experiment](primary-experiment) - the experiment resulting in the ``Overall Results'' in (Section 5.1).
- [Party Order Experiment](party-order-experiment) - the investiation of the effect of reversing the order of parties presented to the Judge (Section 5.3)
- [Bilateral Experiment](bilateral) - comparison of bilateral and trilateral argument settings (Section 5.4)
- [Legal Content vs Rhetorical Form](content-vs-form) - Pitting Advocates against themselves with and without access to original arguments (Section 5.5.1)
- [Human Argument Quality Judgment and Model Persuasion](human-assessment) human assessment of the strengths of arguments (Section 5.5.2)
- [Argument Quantity, Type and Diversity](argument-type) analysis of effect of  argument typologies and quantities on persuasion (Section 5.5.3)
