# Legal Content vs Rhetorical Form

Code, data and stats for investigating the effect of legal content compared to rhetorical form, pitting advocates against themselves with and without access to original arguments (Section 5.5.1).

- `code` - scripts to generate the prompts and the statistics.
- `data` - output from the Judge models, and extracted outcomes from those judgments. Prompts and aggregated outcomes.
- `stats` - statistical analyses of experiment.
